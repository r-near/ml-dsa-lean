# ML-DSA verify: norm check, multiply-accumulate row, lazy NTT, fixed-width w1Encode, 2026-11-07

Branch `fv-p4` (from `fast-verify`). Four verify-side speedups on verify-only arithmetic, each with its Lean proof; Rust and
proof commits are listed below. Built and run by `bench/fast-verify.sh` (nearcore profile and target features; `avx2` = the
dispatched proved path, `v3` = `-C target-cpu=x86-64-v3`). Same machine as [2026-11-07-p3](2026-11-07-p3.md) (AMD EPYC 9B14,
Zen 4, KVM guest, 60 vCPUs, zero steal). Pinned to idle cores (cpu 45 for the default toolchain, rustc 1.99.1; cpu 46 for
1.95.0, nearcore's). The box load was about 1.0 to 1.5 during the runs (about 1 is the pinned benchmark itself; another job
shares the box). Medians of 15 rounds, microseconds.
Raw logs: [`fast-verify-p4.log`](2026-11-07-p4/fast-verify-p4.log) and
[`fast-verify-p4-195.log`](2026-11-07-p4/fast-verify-p4-195.log) (build-tool cache lines and local paths removed).

ML-DSA-65, `avx2` column (the shipping configuration): verify 54.0 (p3, default toolchain) becomes **43.1**; the scalar verify
103.8 becomes 88.2. On 1.95.0 the `avx2` verify is 42.4. `verifying_key` verify 34.0 becomes 32.6. Prepared-key verify
(`ours+prepared-vk`) 23.1 becomes 14.9 (`avx2`) and 20.8 becomes 11.1 (`v3`).
The 43.1 is 1.18 times aws-lc-rs (36.4) on this box (1.17 on 1.95.0: 42.4 against 36.3).

## Notes

- The two toolchain-specific cells of p3 (ML-DSA-65 `v3` on 1.95.0 and ML-DSA-44 `avx2` `ours+verifying-key` on 1.95.0) are
  gone: this run shows 41.9 and 17.6.
- `ours+prepared-vk` in the `avx2` column is the scalar-time prepared verify: the prepared verify does not run inside the AVX2
  wrapper (only the one-shot body does). The `v3` column, where AVX2 is static, shows what dispatching it would give
  (ML-DSA-65: 11.1 against 15.5 on 1.95.0). That needs a second `#[target_feature]` wrapper and was not done.
- Building the key and then verifying (`ours+verifying-key` 32.7 plus `ours+prepared-vk` 15.5 on 1.95.0, 48.2) now costs more than
  a one-shot dispatched verify (42.4).
- The dead-code warnings for the constant-time functions in the bench build are expected: verify calls the `_pub` variants,
  and the constant-time versions stay as the specification the proofs compare against.
